Teen Patti rules

  • Everyone puts in the boot. Play blind (pay the stake) or see your cards (pay double).
  • Raise doubles the stake, up to 16x the boot. Pack to fold.
  • Show (2 players left): pay to compare hands. A tie goes to the opponent.
  • Side show: a seen player asks the previous seen player to compare. The lower hand packs, and a tie packs the asker.
  • Ranking: Trail, Pure sequence, Sequence, Color, Pair, High card. A-K-Q is the top run, A-2-3 is second.
  • Muflis: same hands, but the lowest hand wins.
  • You have 30 seconds per turn, then you pack automatically.
  • Practice: play against bots with the Coach on. It explains your hand and suggests a move. Turn it off when you are ready.
  • You can join a table mid-hand and play from the next hand.
